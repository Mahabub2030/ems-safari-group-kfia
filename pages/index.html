About Safari Group Limited

Safari Group Limited is a specialized contracting company based in Dammam, Saudi Arabia. We deliver high-quality landscaping, irrigation systems, fencing, roads, drainage and waste management services for major projects including King Fahd International Airport (KFIA).

Our experienced team of engineers, supervisors, gardeners and skilled workers ensures excellent execution while maintaining full compliance with Saudi labor and safety regulations.
